## Research standards
1. Keep original source URL, publication date, adoption/effective date (if verified), PDF page number and section.
2. Clearly distinguish adopted ordinance, superseded ordinance, draft, guidance, and field observations.
3. Do not infer that a county-wide road standard automatically applies to a private driveway.
4. Before advising on cabin placement, setbacks, grading, road construction, septic, or water, verify parcel zoning and applicable ordinances with county officials.
5. Archive exact PDFs and checksums when direct source download becomes available. This file is a durable *source index*, not an offline PDF archive.
