# Lanark research library

GitHub is the durable source of truth. Do not treat chat memory, map styling, or unverified interpretations as evidence.

## Structure
- `sources/` — source manifest, original document URLs, publication/adoption dates and checksums where available
- `parcel/` — tax parcel identity, legal descriptions, boundaries and recorded interests
- `roads/` — routes, access permissions, field observations and restrictions
- `terrain/` — elevation, slope, drainage and land-use analysis
- `decisions/` — decision log with source references and unresolved issues

Every conclusion must identify a source, date, verification status and uncertainty. Do not infer legal access from road geometry or federal land-management layers.
