# Lanark 4–8 cabin shared-ownership concept (2026-10-31)

## Owner objectives and constraints
- Bear Lake County PIN RP13S43E185400; 55.75 acres; owner reports Agricultural zoning.
- Develop 4–8 small cabins on **one undivided parcel**.
- Consider an LLC with shared membership interests or tenancy-in-common ownership. An LLC and TIC are different title structures; an LLC can have multiple members while holding sole title.
- Owner reports subdivision is unavailable except possible direct-child transfers. This restriction and any exception require verification against county ordinance, recorded deed and covenants.

## Critical legal/land-use gates
1. Confirm county classification of 4–8 separate cabins: residential dwelling units vs guest/dude ranch accommodations vs campground or other use. Dude ranch appears listed as permitted in Agricultural district under 2024 ordinance, but **that does not automatically authorize multiple cabins**.
2. Determine whether fractional interests, exclusive cabin assignments, transferable memberships, or long-term leases trigger land division, condominium, timeshare, or other review even without separate deeds.
3. Confirm guest ranch operational definition (actual ranch activities, multi-night guest stays, livestock/recreation, dining) and whether proposed use qualifies.
4. Confirm zoning, residential density, fire access, water rights/well, septic capacity, utilities, building permits, roads, setbacks and drainage.
5. Obtain written county preapplication classification **before** transferring title or promising exclusive cabin rights.
6. Obtain Idaho real estate attorney and CPA review of ownership, succession, transfers, liability, financing, insurance and taxes.

## Preliminary alternatives, not approvals
- Single LLC owns all land/cabins; members receive non-deeded contractual use allocations. Simpler centralized management but may still face land-use and securities/tax issues.
- Direct TIC undivided interests with recorded co-ownership agreement. Greater co-owner rights and potential partition/deadlock/financing risk; not a subdivision exemption.
- Genuine guest/dude ranch operated by single owner entity. Only if actual operation meets ordinance definition and county authorizes proposed accommodations.

## Owner clarification — 2026-10-31
Primary intent is **private use by a fixed group of owners/families**. A legitimate business/guest ranch operation is an alternative **only if it offers a clearer lawful approval path**. Do not represent private recreational cabins as a dude ranch without satisfying the county's actual operating definition. Compare (1) private multi-cabin shared LLC use, (2) lawful group recreational lodging, (3) genuine commercial guest/dude ranch. Seek county written land-use classification before selecting title or exclusive use agreements. Verify currently adopted ordinances; county is drafting 2026 updates.
