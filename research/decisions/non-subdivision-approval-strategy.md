# Non-subdivision approval strategy — Lanark (2026-10-31)

## Owner objective
Four to eight individually financed cabins on one undivided 55.75-acre Agricultural parcel. Each LLC/TIC participant receives assigned cabin use and can transfer ownership interest with associated rights. Shared roads and water well. Primary use private; genuine commercial operation only if legally suitable. Owner considers conventional subdivision infeasible.

## Critical finding
Bear Lake County 2024 Land Use Ordinance, Agricultural District: gross residential density one dwelling per 40 acres. https://www.bearlakecounty.info/uploads/2/8/6/7/28674675/bl_county_land_use_ordinance_2024.pdf . This makes 4–8 separate dwelling approvals a high-risk assumption. Idaho Code 50-1301 defines subdivision using lots/parcels/**sites** for sale or building development and allows counties their own definition. https://www.lawserver.com/law/state/idaho/id-code/idaho_code_50-1301 . Single deed/LLC does not automatically avoid review.

## Parallel classification tracks (none approved)
1. Private shared recreational facility / cabin compound: seek county classification of individual cabins, overnight occupancy and assigned sites; test whether each is a dwelling.
2. Legitimate Agricultural accessory ranch lodging: must actually be accessory to a principal permitted ranch operation, not relabeling private homes.
3. Genuine dude/guest ranch: verify ordinance use table, ranch operations, guest lodging definition and whether private assigned transferable use rights are compatible.
4. Other discretionary group recreation / conditional use: identify exact ordinance authority before proposing.

## Required county written determination
- Can 4–8 detached sleeping cabins on one tax parcel be authorized? Under which exact use category, with what maximum occupancy and kitchen/bath limits?
- Does assigning exclusive cabin sites and allowing resale of membership interests constitute land division or regulated unit/site sales?
- Does Agricultural density apply to these structures, and are there lawful exceptions or alternative approvals?
- Does the county recognize private noncommercial multi-cabin use, and what application/appeal procedure applies?
- What fire access, drinking water, sewage, road and building permits are required?

## Stage gates
A: Obtain official written use classification before designing member rights. B: Idaho land-use attorney reviews LLC/TIC transfers and improvements rights. C: Water, septic, road and fire feasibility. D: Site layout and preapplication review. E: Only then finance cabins and transfer interests.

**Do not promise or market separately assigned cabin interests before legal review.** This document is a strategy record, not a determination of legality.
