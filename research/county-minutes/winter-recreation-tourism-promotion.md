## State program
Idaho Parks and Recreation promotes Bear Lake County's snowmobiling network, reports approximately 350 miles of groomed trails, and supports grooming, parking plowing, signs and education. https://parksandrecreation.idaho.gov/park-activity/snowmobiling/

## Interpretation for Lanark
A small legally approved cabin retreat could potentially complement winter trail access and existing regional tourism campaigns. **No verified county statement endorses 4–8 private member-assigned cabins or lodging at the Mill Canyon parking project.** Marketing awards do not pay for private cabin construction automatically. Public recreation access, winter plowing, zoning classification, wastewater/water systems, parking and commercial operations remain unresolved.
