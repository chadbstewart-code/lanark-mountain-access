# Mill Canyon / Mill Farm Road — winter parking and access records

Research date: 2026-11-02. **Commissioners records, not Planning & Zoning approvals.** Do not treat a proposed land exchange or grant as completed without later deeds, approvals and grant records.

## December 9, 2024 — Commissioners
Official PDF: https://www.bearlakecounty.info/uploads/2/8/6/7/28674675/12-09-2024_commissioners_minutes.pdf

Under “SNOWMOBILE PARKING UPDATE”:
- Commissioner Olsen reported proposed exchange of **3 acres county land** for **1.4 acres usable land** involving Liberty Rock and Canyon Vista Ranch LLC, manager Eric Bomsta, for a new fenced snowmobile parking lot farther up Mill Canyon.
- Existing parking area described as ~1/3 mile below proposed location, with an old irrigation ditch.
- Commissioner Payne questioned whether a public hearing would be necessary before the land exchange; no final approval demonstrated in this passage.
- Bomsta described a proposed MOU, with company cash-flowing parking construction, Mill Canyon Road widening/resurfacing, and culvert replacement, contingent on Idaho Department of Parks and Recreation grant for road work.
- Estimated work $70,000–$75,000 and six to nine months (proposal, not verified final costs).
- Bomsta proposed private plowing of ~0.3 mile from existing lot toward Liberty Rock; county would continue plowing to the existing lot. Superintendent Boehme discussed a possible private plowing permit.
- Source passage lines 194–211 in web transcription; consult original PDF for official pagination.
