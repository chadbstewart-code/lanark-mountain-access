## Verified ordinance passages

1. **Chapter 3, sections 1–3 (printed p.24)**: Zoning districts include Agricultural (A), Rural Community (RC), Community Expansion (CE), Multiple Use public lands (MU), Recreation (REC), Rural Conservation (RUC), Lakeshore (LS), Commercial (C), Industrial (I). Official zoning map is controlling, subject to county staff interpretation. Parcel-specific designation **unresolved**.
2. **Chapter 12, Recreational Vehicles (printed p.78)**: Temporary/intermittent recreational RV use is permitted subject to current registration and operability; no attached decks/additions or skirting; not used as a dwelling or rental; separate primary residence required; self-contained tanks with off-site approved disposal or approved sewage system. Construction-related residential RV use has limited exception tied to dwelling construction/permit. **Do not infer unrestricted year-round RV occupancy**.
3. **Chapter 6, section 29 Drainage Ways (printed p.57)**: Where a lot is traversed by drainage/watercourse, ordinance addresses stormwater easement/right-of-way and preservation as open space, with restrictions on roads/structures crossing; alterations require review and may be barred in riparian areas.
4. **Chapter 6 accessory uses (printed p.43)**: Accessory structures generally need building permits, with stated exception for sheds less than 200 sq ft; applicability depends on lawful principal use and other regulations.
5. **Site plan contents (printed p.72-73 vicinity; verify exact section/page before quoting)**: Existing/proposed boundary, easements, drainage and access are requested; some sites with average slope over 8% need grading/topography/erosion plans. **Exact permit applicability unresolved**.
