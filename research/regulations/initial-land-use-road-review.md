## Verified textual extracts (historical 2019 ordinance; applicability unconfirmed)
1. **Definition of access**, PDF p.7 (zero-based PDF page 6): legally and physically defined practical vehicle ingress/egress from an adjoining public road, considering terrain, drainage, erosion and flooding.
2. **Definition of driveway**, PDF pp.11-12 (zero-based PDF pages 10-11): nondedicated private vehicular access serving not more than two dwelling units (excluding ADUs), with minimum 12-foot travel surface and minimum four inches of pit run, gravel or other hardened surface. **Do not apply until checked against 2024 ordinance and applicable fire/road standards.**
3. **Definition of development**, PDF p.11 (zero-based PDF page 10): includes grading, road building, utilities and placement of structures; exclusions include some maintenance activities.
4. **Transportation provision**, PDF pp.5-6 (zero-based PDF pages 4-5): discusses transportation improvements and conditional use for some new road alignments; must check later amendments and specific project applicability.

## Verified road standards extract
Road standards PDF page 13 (zero-based 12): geometric criteria list maximum 10% grade with note that 15% may be allowed with special attention to maintenance. Road cross-section figure on PDF page 17 (zero-based 16) notes a 60-foot minimum right-of-way for its depicted rural road cross-section. These are **not established as requirements for an existing private driveway**.
