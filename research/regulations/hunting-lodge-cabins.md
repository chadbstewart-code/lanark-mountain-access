# Hunting lodge and rental cabins — ordinance finding

Reviewed: 2026-10-31.

Official source: https://www.bearlakecounty.info/uploads/2/8/6/7/28674675/bl_county_land_use_ordinance_2024.pdf

Chapter 4, Table 1, Recreational Uses, printed page 34: **Hunting lodge and rental cabins** has **C** (conditional use) in the Agricultural (A) column. This is a named category distinct from **Dude Ranch** (listed as P, permitted, in Agricultural zoning).

**Not established:** whether noncommercial member-assigned cabins fit this use, permitted cabin count, whether rental activity is required, or whether separately transferable member interests trigger land division or other regulation. Do not treat conditional-use classification as approval.

County 2026 planning update: https://www.bearlakecounty.info/planning-and-zoning.html — verify current adopted text and amendments before filing.

Suggested question for county administrator: Would 4–8 individually funded cabins assigned to transferable LLC interests on one Agricultural parcel be reviewed as hunting lodge/rental cabins, as multiple dwellings, or another use? What evidence of bona fide hunting-lodge operation would be required, and what site, fire, water and septic standards apply?
